# Taskobo Diagrams repository presentation

The repository speaks first to project managers. Lead with the belief that AI
will change project-management tools and release PM time from repetitive document
maintenance for judgment, communication and delivery. Explain the human/AI workflow
and provide a practical starting point before implementation details. Real diagram
output supports that message: every displayed node, bar and responsibility cell
comes from the renderer and fictional, committed source inputs.

Inherit Taskobo's paper, navy and cobalt identity. Use `#FFFEFA` for the page,
`#0D2046` for structural ink, `#1456D9` for emphasis and `#52637A` for supporting
copy. Give each figure open space and a concise caption. Separate major regions
with a hairline instead of rounded cards, decorative backgrounds or gradients.
Typography follows the established Noto Sans JP / Yu Gothic / Meiryo sans stack;
code and exact version data retain monospace. No decorative icon family is needed.

The README may open with one cinematic brand hero under the user's requested
direction: paper objects, navy depth, controlled cobalt light and a short Chinese
message. This is a promotional concept, not a screenshot or evidence of business
output. Keep it separate from the complete renderer figures; do not repeat it in
the gallery. Link Taskobo naturally in a short attribution near the introduction.

The diagrams must prove business depth. Keep the complete six-document CRM case
in `examples/project-case/`: its scope hierarchy, decision loops, integrated variance
schedule, full responsibility matrix, risks and issues share the same scenario.
Never replace those sources with a four-node hierarchy, a few-task schedule or a
three-row responsibility table for visual convenience. The source manifest owns
the facts and counts; the generator reads it without simplifying business content.

Keep the gallery concise: four types only, in WBS, RACI, flow and schedule order.
Each type gets one complete detailed figure, one short description and text links
to the original SVG, PNG, source and optional detail crop. Do not repeat a hero
collage or place a second enlarged detail inline. Risk and issue registers remain
available as complete source and assets; do not expand their text tables column by
column in the presentation. The product name stays modest and the diagrams carry
the page. Complete figures are visible without collapsed disclosures; original-size
links support reading on narrow screens.

Keep the change sequence compact by combining repeated administrative messages,
acknowledgements and notification steps. Preserve the assessment, decision,
implementation and acceptance branches without drawing every retry as a separate
exchange. Shorten the content rather than shrinking the type or stretching images.

Keep full figures and detail crops distinct. All six documents still ship a
complete original SVG and complete PNG. Detail assets retain the original SVG
content and record their viewports; do not pass a crop off as a complete diagram
or pretend that overview labels at thumbnail scale are readable detail.

For late-row table details, keep every role or judgment column. Group whole
columns into reading-width panels and pair the original header viewport with the
same original body-row viewport in each panel. Label source row/column ranges;
do not reconstruct a smaller table or omit Operations, triggers, response or
closure evidence to make the graphic fit. Record each header/body viewBox in the
asset manifest so the relation to the complete SVG is independently inspectable.

New business assets use `docs/assets/crm-case/` so image caches cannot retain the
superseded tiny examples. The previous architecture figure is a technical
integration explanation, not evidence of business-document capability. The RACI
palette comes from the installed renderer: navy accountable, soft blue responsible,
quiet gray consulted/informed and blue-gray combined roles. Do not recolor actual
output or change runtime tokens to decorate the repository.

English is the default public README. English, Japanese and Chinese entries have
matching guides, cinematic covers, four-figure galleries and editable case sources.
Japanese uses plain form. Each language links to the other two. The current
installable PM Skill and continuous-update teaching demo remain Chinese Alpha;
state that boundary explicitly in every guide. Keep the four-type
gallery concise and provide a downloadable starter that PMs can inspect without a
development setup. Technical installation, package versions and integration belong
at the bottom or in linked developer documentation. AI drafting currently happens
in the user's own AI tool; do not imply built-in automatic drafting or cross-document
synchronization. State the current Japanese workbench UI accurately.

The Chinese PM Update Alpha adds an installable workflow under
`skills/taskobo-diagrams`: one main agent reads project facts and new meeting notes,
then local scripts prepare reviewable changes and render WBS, RACI and schedules
from the same candidate. Subagents are optional for independent analysis. Keep
the review interface in plain Chinese, show before/after values and original
evidence, preserve approved baselines and distinguish actual finish dates from
unknown actual start dates. The static preview does not approve changes. State
the local Node/browser requirements and the absence of automatic external-system
connections. Its standalone teaching branch does not revise the original CRM case.

Localized figures preserve the actual renderer's geometry and business values.
Only documentation-layer text and built-in legends are translated. Legend labels
may reflow to fit translated text; business geometry stays fixed. Do not recolor
or stretch the output. Keep locale sources separate for future Japanese and English
editions. Use relative image links for GitHub. State release availability precisely.
No adoption metrics, certification badges, claims of pixel-identical browsers,
fabricated screenshots or automatic deployment promises. Keep marketing images
outside the npm tarball. Source SVGs, inputs and the browser generator remain
reviewable in GitHub.
